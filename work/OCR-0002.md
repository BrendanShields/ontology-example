---
id: OCR-0002
title: Add Calibration to the call-qa glossary
status: draft
bounded_context: call-qa
proposed_term: Calibration
proposed_definition: >-
  The comparison of the agent's Evaluation of a Call with a person's Evaluation of the same
  Call, to measure how far the two agree.
justification: >-
  REQ-0009 and the review step behind it need a name for checking the agent against a person.
  Team leaders already sample Evaluations and compare them. The glossary has no term for the
  comparison or for its result.
---

Open questions for the quality team before this can move to `ready`:

- Is a Calibration one comparison of two Evaluations, or the session in which a batch is compared?
- Does it need its own result, such as an agreement rate, or is that a property of the Scorecard?
