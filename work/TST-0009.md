Bounded exploration, not a proof. A real `EvaluationSession` is driven through every state it can reach within this bound:

- a Scorecard of 1, 2 or 3 Criteria and a Call of two Utterances
- in each state, 43 to 79 proposals: every Criterion and one unknown one, every outcome, six ways of citing Utterances, and seven things that are not a Finding at all
- in each state, an attempt to finish

That is 438 states and 34,014 steps. Each step is checked against properties G1 to G7 in `apps/call-qa-agent/tests/bounded/explore.py`, written from the sentences of the Requirements. A fault that needs four or more Criteria, or a proposal outside that list, is outside the bound.
