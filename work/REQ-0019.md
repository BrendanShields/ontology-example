  When a `Review` with the `review_outcome` `OVERTURNED` is made of an `Evaluation` whose
  `publication_state` is `STAGED` and whose `disposition` is `NEEDS_REVIEW`, the call-qa-agent
  shall give the `Evaluation` the `publication_state` `WITHHELD`.
