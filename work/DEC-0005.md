id: DEC-0005
title: Leave Criteria that do not apply out of the score
status: done
bounded_context: call-qa
rationale: >-
  A Call with no complaint was losing the weight of the complaint-handling Criterion. A NOT_APPLICABLE
  Finding now counts neither for nor against: the score is the weight met as a percentage
  of the weight that applied, rounded down. If nothing applied, the score is 0 and a person
  reviews the Evaluation.
uses_terms: [score, weight, Finding, NOT_APPLICABLE]
supersedes: DEC-0004
decided_by: call-qa squad with the quality team
decided_on: '2026-10-06'
