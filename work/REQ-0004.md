---
id: REQ-0004
title: The score is computed from the Findings
status: done
bounded_context: call-qa
jira_key: CQA-104
ears_text: >-
  The call-qa-agent shall compute the `score` of an `Evaluation` as the total `weight` of
  every `Criterion` whose `Finding` is `MET`, as a percentage of the total `weight` of every
  `Criterion` whose `Finding` is not `NOT_APPLICABLE`, rounded down.
uses_terms: [score, Evaluation, weight, Criterion, Finding, MET, NOT_APPLICABLE]
risk_tier: medium
---

See DEC-0005 for why Criteria that do not apply are left out, and DEC-0006 for why the model is never asked for the score.
