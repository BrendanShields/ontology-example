  If the completion of an `Evaluation` is asked for while a `Criterion` of the `Scorecard`
  has no `Finding`, then the call-qa-agent shall answer with one `MISSING_FINDING` `Rejection`
  for each such `Criterion` and not complete the `Evaluation`.
