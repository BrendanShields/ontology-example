Bounded exploration, not a proof. The real `scoring.score` and `scoring.conclude` are run on every case within this bound:

- a Scorecard of 1, 2 or 3 Criteria
- each Criterion with a weight of 1, 2 or 3, critical or not
- one Finding per Criterion, with every combination of outcomes
- every pass mark from 0 to 100

That is 623,574 Evaluations. For REQ-0010 it is also run on 388 lists of Findings, of which 9 are one per Criterion: every list of up to one Finding more than the Criteria, over the Criteria and one unknown one. Each case is checked against properties S1 to S7 in `apps/call-qa-agent/tests/bounded/explore.py`, written from the sentences of the Requirements. A fault that needs four or more Criteria or a weight above 3 to show is outside the bound.
