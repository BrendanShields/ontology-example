---
id: REQ-0010
ears_text: >-
  If the `findings` given for an `Evaluation` are not exactly one `Finding` for each `Criterion`
  of the `Scorecard`, then the call-qa-agent shall produce no `Evaluation`.
uses_terms: [findings, Evaluation, Finding, Criterion, Scorecard]
risk_tier: medium
title: No Evaluation from duplicate or missing Findings
status: done
bounded_context: call-qa
---

REQ-0001 keeps duplicate and missing Findings out through the write path. This Requirement holds the domain layer to the same thing when it is called another way: `scoring.conclude` used to return a score of 100, PASS and FINAL for two Findings on one Criterion of two. It now raises a `ValueError`. No `Rejection` is involved, because nobody is there to correct a proposal.
