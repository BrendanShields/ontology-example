The state machine is `apps/call-qa-agent/src/call_qa_agent/domain/publication.py`: pure functions over the generated types. `apps/call-qa-agent/docs/publication.md` is drawn from runs of it.

| From | Asked | Guard | To |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| not staged | stage | the Evaluation is complete | STAGED |
| STAGED | publish | the disposition is FINAL, or a Review of this Call and Scorecard CONFIRMED it | PUBLISHED |
| STAGED | publish | otherwise | refused with a REVIEW_REQUIRED Rejection |
| STAGED | Review, OVERTURNED | the disposition is not FINAL | WITHHELD |
| STAGED | Review, CONFIRMED | the disposition is not FINAL | STAGED, and publishing is now permitted |
| STAGED | Review | the disposition is FINAL | refused |
| PUBLISHED, WITHHELD | anything | | refused |

What was chosen where the request left room:

- **One table, one maker.** `CHANGES` lists every change of PublicationState, and `_enter` is the only function that makes an AuditRecord and it consults the table. A change that is not in the table cannot be recorded by any path.
- **A CONFIRMED Review is not a change of state.** There are three PublicationStates and "confirmed" is not one of them. The Review is recorded and no AuditRecord is.
- **A second Review is taken while the Evaluation is STAGED.** Another reviewer may confirm as well, and an OVERTURNED Review after a CONFIRMED one withholds the Evaluation. Withholding is the safe direction, so the later doubt wins. Once it is PUBLISHED no Review is taken.
- **Fail closed on the disposition.** The guard is "not FINAL", so a disposition added to the glossary later needs a Review until someone decides otherwise.
- **Only REVIEW_REQUIRED is a Rejection.** It is the one refusal the proposer can do something about, and the one RejectionRule the glossary has for publication. A Review of a FINAL Evaluation, and anything asked of a PUBLISHED or WITHHELD one, is refused by raising `Refused` with a reason and no Rejection, as REQ-0010 does. No rule was coined for them.

Not decided here, and a person should: who may be a reviewer. The reviewer is an identifier the caller gives, and nothing checks it.
