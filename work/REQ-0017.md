ears_text: >-
  If the publication of an `Evaluation` is asked for while its `disposition` is `NEEDS_REVIEW`
  and no `Review` with the `review_outcome` `CONFIRMED` exists for its `Call` and `Scorecard`,
  then the call-qa-agent shall answer with a `REVIEW_REQUIRED` `Rejection` and leave its `publication_state`
  `STAGED`.
