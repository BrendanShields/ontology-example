The three cases, each in the bounded exploration of TST-0018:

| Asked | While the ledger holds | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| stage the same Evaluation again | it, in any PublicationState | nothing is recorded, and that is not an error |
| stage a different Evaluation of the same Call against the same Scorecard | another, STAGED | refused |
| the same | another, PUBLISHED or WITHHELD | refused |

"The same" is decided by a SHA-256 of the Evaluation, kept on the staging line of the ledger. The digest is not a glossary term and is not in any generated type: it lives in the adapter (`ledger.py`) only. Without it, two Evaluations of one Call with the same disposition could not be told apart, and a Review of one would publish the other.

What this costs, and a person should weigh:

- An Evaluation that a Review OVERTURNED can never be replaced by a better one for the same Call and Scorecard. WITHHELD is final in the glossary, and the glossary gives an Evaluation no identifier of its own. If the quality team wants a withheld Call evaluated again, that is an OntologyChangeRequest: an identifier for an Evaluation, or a way out of WITHHELD.
- Evaluating the same Call twice with a live model gives two different Evaluations, so the second `--stage` is refused. That is the point, and it means the output of the first run has to be kept by whoever ran it.
