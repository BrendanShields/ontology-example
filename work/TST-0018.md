Bounded exploration, not a proof. The real `Ledger` is driven on a real file through every sequence of commands within this bound:

- one Call and one Scorecard, and four Evaluations of them built by the real `scoring.conclude`: two that are FINAL and two that are NEEDS_REVIEW, one by each cause (a failed critical Criterion, and no Criterion applied)
- two reviewers
- nine commands: stage each of the four Evaluations, each reviewer's Review with each ReviewOutcome, and publish
- every sequence of 0 to 5 commands, and every command given a second time straight after the first
- after each staging, the twin of the Evaluation staged is offered once: the same outcome for each Criterion, so the same score and disposition, with Findings that cite another Utterance. It has to be refused (DEC-0012). It is not one of the nine commands, so the counts below do not include it

That is 66,430 sequences and 66,429 commands. Each is checked against properties P1 to P13 in `apps/call-qa-agent/tests/bounded/explore.py`, written from REQ-0016 to REQ-0025 and DEC-0012. Each property has at least one planted fault in `mutants.py` that it must report.

Outside the bound: a second Call or Scorecard on the same ledger (`test_publication.py` and `test_ledger.py` have examples), a sequence of six or more commands, two writers at once (`test_ledger.py::TestGivenAgain` gives each command twice at once), a crash in the middle of a write, and a ledger file someone edited (`test_ledger.py::TestALedgerNobodyWrote` has examples of both). The oracle is one reading of the Requirements.
