ears_text: >-
  When the publication of an `Evaluation` is asked for while its `publication_state` is `STAGED`
  and either its `disposition` is `FINAL` or a `Review` of it has the `review_outcome` `CONFIRMED`,
  the call-qa-agent shall give it the `publication_state` `PUBLISHED`.
uses_terms: [Evaluation, publication_state, STAGED, disposition, FINAL, Review, review_outcome,
  CONFIRMED, PUBLISHED]
risk_tier: high
title: >-
  A staged Evaluation that stands, or that a Review confirmed, is published when asked
status: done
bounded_context: call-qa
