---
id: REQ-0009
title: Scores under review are not published
status: superseded
bounded_context: call-qa
ears_text: >-
  While an `Evaluation` has the `disposition` `NEEDS_REVIEW`, the call-qa-agent shall not
  publish its `score` to the `Representative`.
uses_terms: [Evaluation, disposition, NEEDS_REVIEW, score, Representative]
risk_tier: high
---

Superseded by REQ-0016 before it was ever agreed. As written it would forbid publishing an Evaluation that a Review had CONFIRMED, because a Review does not change the disposition: the Evaluation is still NEEDS_REVIEW when it is published. OCR-0005 gave the review step its own terms, Review and PublicationState, so it no longer waits on OCR-0002.
