title: One Finding per Criterion
status: done
bounded_context: call-qa
jira_key: CQA-101
ears_text: >-
  The call-qa-agent shall record exactly one `Finding` for each `Criterion` of the `Scorecard`
  an `Evaluation` applies.
uses_terms: [Finding, Criterion, Scorecard, Evaluation]
risk_tier: medium
---

No Criterion may be skipped and none may be invented. The write path rejects a Finding for an unknown Criterion and a second Finding for the same one, and an Evaluation cannot be completed while a Criterion has no Finding.
